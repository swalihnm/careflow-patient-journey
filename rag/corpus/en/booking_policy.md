# Gulf Horizon Clinics -- Booking, Rescheduling and Cancellation Policy

## Booking an appointment

Appointments can be booked through the CareFlow assistant, by phone, or in
person at either clinic (Downtown Dubai or Marina). New patients should
arrive 15 minutes before their first appointment to complete registration.

## Rescheduling

Appointments can be rescheduled up to 2 hours before the scheduled time at
no charge, subject to slot availability. Rescheduling within 2 hours of the
appointment is treated as a late cancellation (see below).

## Cancellation

Cancellations made more than 24 hours before the appointment incur no fee.
Cancellations made between 2 and 24 hours before the appointment incur a fee
of AED 50. Cancellations made less than 2 hours before the appointment, or
missed appointments ("no-shows"), incur a fee of AED 150, unless the
appointment was for a red-flag emergency escalation.

## Reminders

Patients receive appointment reminders by SMS. High no-show-risk
appointments may also receive a second reminder message on the day of the
appointment, as part of an ongoing reminder-effectiveness study (see the
Reminder Effectiveness Notice).

## Walk-ins

Walk-in patients are seen on a best-effort basis after all scheduled
patients for that time block. Walk-ins are not guaranteed to be seen the
same day.

## Clinic hours

- Downtown Dubai: Sunday-Thursday 8:00-20:00, Friday-Saturday 9:00-17:00
- Marina: Sunday-Thursday 8:00-20:00, Friday-Saturday 9:00-17:00

Public holidays: both clinics are closed on UAE public holidays; emergency
cases should call 998 (ambulance) or go to the nearest hospital.
