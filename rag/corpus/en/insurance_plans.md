# Gulf Horizon Insurance -- Plan Coverage Guide

Three mock plans, used to answer patient coverage questions. These are
fictional plans authored for this project, not real Gulf Horizon Insurance
products.

## Gulf Horizon Standard

- General practice and family medicine visits: covered in full after a AED 50 co-payment.
- Specialist visits: covered at 80% after a referral from a general practitioner; no referral means 50% coverage.
- Physiotherapy: covered at 70%, up to 10 sessions per year.
- Diagnostic imaging (X-ray, ultrasound): covered at 80%.
- MRI/CT scans: covered at 60%, pre-authorisation required.
- Maternity care: not covered under Standard; see Gulf Horizon Family.
- Annual out-of-pocket maximum: AED 15,000.

## Gulf Horizon Plus

- General practice and family medicine visits: covered in full, no co-payment.
- Specialist visits: covered at 90%, no referral required.
- Physiotherapy: covered at 80%, up to 20 sessions per year.
- Diagnostic imaging: covered at 90%.
- MRI/CT scans: covered at 80%, pre-authorisation required for non-emergency scans.
- Maternity care: covered at 70% after a 10-month waiting period from policy start.
- Dental and vision: basic coverage included, up to AED 2,000/year combined.
- Annual out-of-pocket maximum: AED 8,000.

## Gulf Horizon Family

- Everything in Gulf Horizon Plus, plus:
- Maternity care: covered at 90% after a 6-month waiting period from policy start.
- Paediatric visits: covered in full, no co-payment, no referral required.
- Well-child checks and standard childhood vaccinations: covered in full.
- Family physiotherapy pool: up to 40 sessions per year, shared across enrolled family members.
- Annual out-of-pocket maximum: AED 10,000 per family.

## General notes that apply to all three plans

- Pre-authorisation is required for any planned hospital admission.
- Emergency care is covered regardless of pre-authorisation; notify the
  insurer within 48 hours of an emergency admission.
- Waiting periods apply only to maternity care; all other covered services
  are available from the policy start date.
- None of these plans cover cosmetic procedures or experimental treatments.
