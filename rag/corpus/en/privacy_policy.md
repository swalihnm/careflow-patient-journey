# Gulf Horizon Clinics -- Privacy and Consent Policy

## What we collect

Gulf Horizon Clinics collects the demographic, contact, appointment, and
clinical information necessary to provide care, process insurance coverage,
and communicate with patients. The CareFlow assistant only reads the
information needed to book appointments, answer coverage questions, and
record pre-visit intake -- it does not have access to diagnoses or
medication records.

## Consent

Patients consent separately to: (1) AI-assisted intake and booking through
the CareFlow assistant, and (2) sharing records with another Gulf Horizon
Clinics location through the health information exchange, when clinically
relevant (for example, a discharge summary shared with a follow-up clinic).
Either consent can be withdrawn at any time through the patient app or by
asking staff; withdrawing consent does not affect the right to receive care.

## Sharing between clinics

With consent, clinically relevant documents (such as discharge summaries)
may be shared between the Downtown Dubai and Marina locations so that a
patient's care team has the context they need. Every such share is logged
in an auditable record. Sharing never happens without an active consent on
file for that purpose.

## Access and audit

Every access to a patient's record -- by a clinician, by the assistant's
booking tools, or by an auditor -- is logged. Patients may request a summary
of who has accessed their record.

## Data retention

Clinical records are retained per UAE healthcare record-keeping
requirements. Conversation transcripts with the assistant are retained for
quality review and are access-controlled; personally identifying details
are masked before any transcript is used for that review.
